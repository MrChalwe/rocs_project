SLACEN Project: Strengthening Lifelong Access to Care and Early Nutrition

2023 – Ongoing ROCS Team 5 min read

The Strengthening Lifelong Access to Care and Early Nutrition (SLACEN) project is a holistic early childhood development initiative in Vubwi District, Eastern Province. Since 2023, SLACEN has constructed 36 child-friendly Early Childhood Education (ECE) centres with play parks, benefiting over 7,500 children.

Project Overview

SLACEN supports children aged 0–6 years to grow up healthy, protected, and ready to learn, especially in communities affected by poverty, climate shocks, food insecurity, and limited access to early learning services. The project integrates early learning, nutrition, health, and child protection through a coordinated, community-led approach.

Geographic Coverage

Vubwi District, Eastern Province

What the Project Does

  • Expanding access to quality ECE through the construction of child-friendly centres and satellite learning spaces equipped with sanitation facilities, play parks, and teaching/learning materials.
  • Building teacher and caregiver capacity through training, coaching, and mentoring on play-based learning, inclusive education, positive discipline, and safe learning environments.
  • Strengthening child health and nutrition through feeding initiatives, nutrition demonstrations, school gardens, and linkages with Under-Five Clinics and community health workers.
  • Promoting child protection and safeguarding, including positive parenting, prevention of violence, and strengthened reporting and referral systems.
  • Engaging parents and communities through structured sessions, dialogue meetings, and active community committees to enhance ownership and sustainability.
  • Coordinating with government departments to align services and strengthen long-term systems.

Children at the Centre

Children actively participate in learning and protection awareness activities using play, storytelling, songs, drawings, and role play. Caregivers, teachers, and parents are trained to listen to and respond to children’s needs, ensuring services remain responsive, inclusive, and child-centred.

Sustainability and Long-Term Impact

SLACEN prioritises community ownership of ECE centres, strengthened local capacity, and integration of successful approaches into government systems. By aligning with national policies in education, health, and child protection, the project lays strong foundations for lifelong learning, dignity, and resilience for children in Vubwi District.

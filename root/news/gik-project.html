GIK Project: Strengthening Literacy through Gift in Kind Books

2024 – Ongoing ROCS Team Supported by: ChildFund Zambia

The GIK (Gift in Kind) Project provides supplementary reading materials to strengthen literacy and improve learning environments in rural schools across Eastern and Northern Provinces.

Overview

Implemented with support from ChildFund Zambia, the project distributes quality-assured, age-appropriate books to encourage a reading culture, engage learners, and enhance education quality in underserved communities.

Geographic Coverage

Eastern Province: Sinda, Petauke, Lusangazi, Katete
Northern Province: Kasama, Mbala

Beneficiaries & Reach

  • Primary and secondary school learners, tertiary students, teachers, and school communities in six districts
  • 16,798 quality-assured books distributed
  • 41,933 books screened for suitability and alignment with National values

Key Focus Areas

  • Literacy development and fostering a reading culture
  • Access to supplementary learning materials
  • Strengthening school libraries and learning spaces
  • Improving learner engagement and education quality

Impact So Far

  • Greater access to age-appropriate, value-aligned reading materials
  • Increased learner interest in reading and self-guided learning
  • Reported improvements in reading skills, including among Grade 7 learners
  • Strengthened school libraries and learning spaces
  • At Luswepo Primary School in Mbala District, GIK books transformed the library into an active learning hub. Teachers and parents noted significant improvements in learners’ reading abilities and confidence.

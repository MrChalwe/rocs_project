WANU II Project: Promoting Community Resiliency through Adaptation of WASH, Health and Nutrition Solutions

2021 – Ongoing ROCS Team Supported by: ChildFund Zambia

The WANU II Project strengthens community resilience by promoting adaptive solutions that improve health, food security, and socioeconomic wellbeing in vulnerable communities affected by cholera, floods, droughts, and rising costs of essentials.

Where the Project Works

Implemented in Chama District (Eastern Province) and Mafinga District (Muchinga Province), the project targets:

  • 96 communities
  • 24 community schools

Project Approach

WANU II promotes integrated interventions addressing health, water access, nutrition, and climate resilience. It supports improved hygiene behaviours, safe drinking water, sanitation services, and community awareness campaigns to reduce disease outbreaks and improve health conditions for children and women.

Climate Resilience and Sustainable Livelihoods

In response to climate change impacts such as droughts, floods, and changing rainfall patterns, the project promotes sustainable adaptation strategies including:

  • Promoting irrigation and efficient water use
  • Supporting climate-smart agriculture and crop diversification
  • Encouraging drought-resistant crops
  • Promoting afforestation and environmental conservation
  • Strengthening community capacity to manage food, water, and natural resources sustainably

Strengthening Community Structures

The project works closely with community structures, local stakeholders, and government institutions to strengthen service delivery, advocacy, and sustainable development.

Gender Equality and Inclusion

WANU II ensures inclusive participation, particularly for women, children, the elderly, and people with disabilities, using non-discriminatory approaches to provide safe and fair access to all project activities.

Human Rights-Based Approach

Implemented using a Human Rights-Based Approach (HRBA), the project raises awareness among duty bearers and empowers rights holders to claim their rights to safe water, sanitation, health, food, and education while encouraging community participation and accountability.

Your support helps vulnerable communities in Chama and Mafinga build resilience against climate shocks and improve health, nutrition, and WASH services.

School Readiness Initiative (SRI): Preparing Children and Schools for Success

2019 – Ongoing ROCS Team Supported by: ChildFund Zambia

The School Readiness Initiative (SRI) is a flagship ROCS programme that ensures children are ready for school and schools are ready for children. It strengthens early learning systems and supports smooth transitions into primary education for vulnerable and rural children.

Goal

To enhance children’s school readiness by strengthening early learning environments, caregiver and teacher capacity, parental engagement, and community ownership of early education.

Our Impact So Far

  • 99,169 children supported with improved early learning opportunities
  • 2,333 teachers and caregivers trained in quality ECCDE delivery
  • Active implementation in 22 districts across Eastern, Northern, and Muchinga Provinces

Key Focus Areas

  • Capacity building for early learning teachers and caregivers
  • Training of Parents Community School Committees (PCSCs), Parent Management Committees (PMCs), and district education structures
  • Promotion of child-centred, play-based learning approaches
  • Strengthening parenting skills, including early stimulation, nurturing care, nutrition, and child health
  • Development and use of age-appropriate teaching and learning materials
  • Support to welcoming, inclusive, and safe school environments

Target Groups

  • Pre-school aged children, especially Orphans and Vulnerable Children (OVCs)
  • Early learning teachers and caregivers
  • Parents and caregivers
  • Community school management structures
  • District and school-level education officials

Partners

Reformed Open Community Schools (ROCS), Zambia Open Community Schools (ZOCS), Roger Federer Foundation (RFF), and the Ministry of General Education.

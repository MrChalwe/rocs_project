AVIEW-GEP: Amplifying Voices for Inclusive and Climate-Resilient WASH Governance

2025 ROCS Team Supported by: ChildFund Zambia

The AVIEW-GEP project strengthens inclusive, climate-resilient WASH governance in Eastern Province. By empowering communities, schools, and local leaders, the project ensures that water, sanitation, and hygiene services meet the needs of all people — especially women, girls, youth, and persons with disabilities.

Programme Overview

Through participatory planning, policy dialogue, and behaviour change approaches, AVIEW-GEP promotes sustainable and accountable WASH services in Chipata and Vubwi districts.

Geographic Coverage

Eastern Province – Chipata and Vubwi Districts

Beneficiaries

  • 60 community and school WASH committees supported
  • 120 community leaders, women, youth, and persons with disabilities trained in inclusive governance
  • 60 schools and communities engaged in sanitation and hygiene promotion
  • Indirect beneficiaries: Rural learners, teachers, and community members in Chipata and Vubwi, including marginalized groups

Key Interventions

  • Facilitation of multi-stakeholder policy dialogue forums at provincial and district levels
  • Establishment and strengthening of WASH committees with inclusive representation
  • Capacity building on inclusive WASH planning, rights, and advocacy
  • Development/revision of local WASH action plans aligned to national frameworks
  • Promotion of sanitation and hygiene behaviours through participatory approaches
  • Documentation of good practices and cross-learning visits to foster scalable innovations

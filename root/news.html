SLACEN: Strengthening Lifelong Access to Care and Early Nutrition

SLACEN is a holistic early childhood development initiative in Vubwi District. Since 2023, the project has built 36 child-friendly ECE centres with play parks, reaching over 7,500 young children with integrated early learning, nutrition, health, and child protection support.

Towards Malaria Elimination (TME) Project
